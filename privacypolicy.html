Privacy Policy

Last Updated: 26/07/2026

Welcome to the official website of John Westley Turnbull. Your privacy is important to us. This policy explains how we handle information on this site.

1. Information We Collect

We use Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity to understand how visitors interact with our site (e.g., which pages are most popular and what country visitors are from). This data is anonymous and does not identify you personally.

This site uses cookies through Google and Microsoft to provide its services and analyse traffic. You can control or reset your cookies through your web browser settings at any time.

3. External Links (Amazon and Other Retailers)

As an author, this site contains links to Amazon and other retailers where my books are available for purchase. Please note that once you click these links, you are subject to the privacy policy and terms of service of the relevant retailer.

4. Contact Information

If you choose to contact us via email or the form, we will only use your information to respond to your inquiry. We will never sell or share your personal details with third parties.
