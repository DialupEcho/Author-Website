MISSION BRIEF // ORBITAL FLIGHT

Chart the drift.

VTR-16 is a pointer-driven space flight game about momentum, precision landings, and finding a route through a living orbital system.

Launch VTR-16

STEER

Click and hold to point the ship toward the pointer and build thrust. Release to coast through space.

LAND

Touch a planet and the ship settles onto its expanded edge. Drag while landed to take off again.

AUTO FLIGHT

Engage AUTO to visit random planets, route around the sun, land, wait five seconds, and continue.
